Get to know the conversation.

AI is not one single thing. These starting points can help make discussions more informed, balanced, and useful.

⌘

What do we mean by AI?

Artificial intelligence describes computer systems designed to perform tasks that typically require human-like abilities, such as recognizing patterns or generating language.

✦

Where can it help?

AI can support people with repetitive work, accessibility, research, creative exploration, and finding patterns in large amounts of information.

◉

What should we question?

Bias, privacy, inaccurate outputs, job disruption, and who benefits are real concerns. Context and human oversight matter.
